The Problem

1.3 Billion Tonnes Wasted

Every year. Worth over ₹92,000 crore in India alone. And it starts in our own kitchens.

Who Is Affected

Households and families whose kitchens pile up with groceries but have no central record of purchase or expiry dates.

The Current Gap

Printed dates are small, faded, or easy to misread. No reminder system means items are only noticed once they've already spoiled.

Why It Matters Now

Almost no household has an easy way to track expiry dates. Still-usable near-expiry products get thrown away instead of shared.

Solution

Four Pillars of Fresh Track

Scan, calculate, notify, and share — all from your browser, no app install needed.

OCR Smart Scan

Capture or upload a label; Tesseract.js reads the printed date automatically in your browser.

Auto Expiry Calc

Manufacture date + shelf life instantly computes the exact expiry date for any product.

Smart Notifications

Automatic alerts as items approach their expiry window — never caught off guard again.

Donate Near-Expiry

List still-good items so nearby users can claim them before they go to waste.

Unique Selling Point

Combines in-browser OCR expiry tracking with a built-in donation marketplace and demand analytics — turning waste prevention into actual redistribution, not just reminders.
